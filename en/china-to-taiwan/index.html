Send money from China to Taiwan: fees, exchange rates and delivery times

Using 20,000 CNY as an example, this page compares 3 ways to send money from China to Taiwan. HSBC Global Transfers delivers the most (about 93,974 TWD) and is also the fastest (near-instant). Change the amount, filter by purpose, or open a method to see how its cost is calculated. Figures were compiled on October 5, 2026 and are reference values.

Reference rate: 1 Chinese yuan (CNY) ≈ 4.73 TWD (rate of October 5, 2026; not updated automatically)

Individuals in China have an annual foreign-exchange purchase quota (USD 50,000 equivalent per person), and most online transfer platforms cannot send money out of China, so the options are limited.
